# 按表达功能改写：原创演练

目录：目的；文献综合；方法；结果；语气；因果；段落承接；摘要；定义与分类；致谢；词汇。全部情境与数字为本技能编写的合成演练，不是文献或用户实验事实。以下展示思路，不是唯一标准答案；科学内容若在真实稿中没有依据，就不新增。

## E01 研究目的：把空泛名词结构换成明确动作

原文：The aim of this study is the carrying out of a comparison of adsorption on flat and curved carbon surfaces.

修改：This study compares adsorption on flat and curved carbon surfaces.

作用：保留比较对象和目的；没有添加novel、first或未给出的优势。来源功能：Introducing Work。

## E02 文献综合：比较轴来自已有内容

原文：Study A measured adsorption from solution [12]. Study B imaged adsorbed molecules [13]. Study C calculated binding energies [14]. These studies studied adsorption.

修改：These studies characterize adsorption through complementary measurements and calculations: solution measurements [12], imaging of adsorbed molecules [13], and binding-energy calculations [14].

作用：以证据类型建立关系，保留三项归属；未声称三者测量同一量、得出一致结果或已验证某机制。来源功能：Reviewing the Literature；Comparing and Contrasting。

## E03 方法：清楚但不删条件

原文：The adsorption experiment was conducted by the mixing of CNHs and a 0.05 mmol/L methanolic solution at 25 °C for 30 min. Three independent experiments were performed.

修改：CNHs were mixed with a 0.05 mmol/L methanolic solution at 25 °C for 30 min. Three independent experiments were performed.

作用：保留材料、浓度、溶剂、温度、时长与独立次数；不补充未给的搅拌、超声或误差定义。来源功能：Describing Methods。

## E04 结果：给数值，不自动加显著性

原文：There was an increase of the conversion from 40% to 55% under the same conditions (Fig. 3b).

修改：The conversion increased from 40% to 55% under the same conditions (Fig. 3b).

作用：保留数值与图号；如用户要求量化差异，可另说明增加15个百分点，或相对增加37.5%，并明确口径。没有统计检验就不改成statistically significant。来源功能：Reporting Results；Describing Quantities。

## E05 观察与解释：只限定真正不确定的部分

原文：It may possibly appear that two configurations may have been observed at 60 kV. These configurations may perhaps reflect different adsorption geometries.

前提：作者已确认两种构型确被观察到，仅其吸附几何解释待检验。

修改：Two configurations were observed at 60 kV. They may reflect different adsorption geometries.

作用：观察直接表述，解释保留一个必要限定。若“是否观察到”本身尚未确认，不能照抄这个修改。来源功能：Discussing Findings；Being Cautious。

## E06 因果：区分句法修改与论断变更

原文：The correlation between loading and current proves that the higher loading causes the higher current.

已有材料：只给相关性分析，没有因果识别或干预证据。

建议版本：Loading was correlated with current.

处理：这是涉及科学论断的修正，须标明由因果主张改为相关关系的原因；若任务仅准校对，保留原句的科学主张，单列作者核查建议。仅添加may或we believe不能解决论证缺口。来源功能：Explaining Causality；通用信息保护约定。

## E07 段落衔接：接住具体对象

原文：The molecules adopted two configurations. This was different. One configuration had a longer residence time than the other.

修改：The molecules adopted two configurations with different residence times: one configuration persisted longer than the other.

作用：用已有比较消除空泛this，未把持续更久等同于更高吸附能。来源功能：Signalling Transition；Paragraph Structure。

## E08 综述摘要：写综合工作，不伪造实验

原文：This review provides a discussion of how curvature and molecular planarity affect adsorption. It provides a comparison of imaging and computational evidence.

修改：This review examines how curvature and molecular planarity affect adsorption and compares imaging with computational evidence.

作用：保留综述动作与范围，没有变成We experimentally demonstrate，也不补造性能数字。来源功能：Writing Abstracts。

## E09 定义与分类：把标准说清楚

原文：Here, capture refers to the immobilization of molecules on CNHs. The samples are divided according to the location of the molecules into tip-associated and sidewall-associated samples.

修改：Here, capture means immobilizing molecules on CNHs. Samples are classified by molecular location as tip-associated or sidewall-associated.

作用：明确本稿定义和分类标准；真实体系若存在第三类或类别交叉，应按原始分类处理，不能因这条例子而强制二分。来源功能：Defining Terms；Classifying and Listing。

## E10 致谢：只整理真实贡献

原文：We would like to thank Dr Lee because Dr Lee helped with the image analysis.

修改：We thank Dr Lee for assistance with image analysis.

作用：保留人名和贡献；不增加经费项目、合作单位、作者资格或资助声明。来源功能：Writing Acknowledgements。

## E11 词汇：以句义和词性判断

原文：The revised protocol was designed to effect a change in surface coverage.

处理：effect a change在此语法成立。若用户仅要求校对，可以保留；若要求精简，可改为The revised protocol was designed to change surface coverage。

作用：不按affect动词/effect名词的简化口诀误改句意。词典核查来源见phrasebank-sources。
